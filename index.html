Years Ahead

An opportunity for students to learn directly from practitioners who built the first generation of digital government.

Date Friday, March 27
Time 1:00 – 5:00 PM
Location Annenberg Auditorium, Weill Hall, University of Michigan
About

Years Ahead brings together pioneering civic technologists, people who led digital service teams at 18F, USDS, Code for America, and state and local governments, with students entering the field. An afternoon of candid conversation about what actually happened in the first wave of government modernization and what the next generation needs to know.

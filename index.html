Years Ahead

A conference for the next generation of public service delivery.

Spring 2025 Event

March 27, 2025 • 1:00 - 4:30 PM

University of Michigan

Join us for an afternoon of open dialogue between students and practitioners. The event features panel discussions with working professionals, fireside conversations, and open discussions exploring the past, present, and future of public service delivery. The afternoon concludes with a student lightning talk session where attendees can share their own projects and ideas.

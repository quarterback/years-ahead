Years Ahead

An opportunity for students to learn directly from practitioners who built the first generation of digital government.

Date Friday, March 27
Time 1:00 – 5:00 PM
Location Ford School, U-M
About

Years Ahead brings together pioneering civic technologists—people who led digital service teams at 18F, USDS, Code for America, and state/local governments—with students entering the field. An afternoon of candid conversation about what actually happened in the first wave of government modernization and what the next generation needs to know.
